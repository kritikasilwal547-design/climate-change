FINDING SOLUTIONS

Solutions to Climate Change

We can help slow climate change by changing the way we use energy, travel and protect nature.

WHAT CAN WE DO?

Small Actions Can Help

Everyone can help protect the planet. Simple actions can reduce pollution and save natural resources.

Use Clean Energy

Solar and wind energy can reduce the use of fossil fuels and lower pollution.

Plant Trees

Trees absorb carbon dioxide and help keep the air cleaner.

Save Energy

Turning off lights and using less electricity can help reduce energy waste.

Reduce Waste

Reusing, recycling and reducing plastic can help protect the environment.

OUR RESPONSIBILITY

Protect Our Planet

When people work together, we can reduce the effects of climate change and create a healthier future.
