WHAT IS HAPPENING?

Effects of Climate Change

Climate change affects our environment, wildlife, communities and daily lives in many different ways.

Floods and Heavy Rainfall

Climate change can increase the intensity of some rainfall events. Heavy rainfall can cause floods, damage homes and roads, and affect people's lives.

Melting Glaciers

Rising temperatures cause glaciers and ice to melt. This can change water supplies and contribute to rising sea levels.

Drought and Extreme Heat

Higher temperatures can make heatwaves more severe and increase the risk of drought in some regions. This can affect water supplies, crops and ecosystems.

Effects on Living Things

Climate change can affect animals, plants and people. Changes in temperature, rainfall and habitats can make it harder for some species to survive and adapt.
